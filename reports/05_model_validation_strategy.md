## 1. Task-Specific Validation Architectures & Evaluation Metrics

To guarantee that models generalize beyond training data and survive the Module 5 unseen evaluation scenario, each task enforces a strict validation protocol where **all preprocessing transformers (`SimpleImputer`, `StandardScaler`, `RobustScaler`, `OneHotEncoder`, `TfidfVectorizer`) are fit exclusively inside the training fold (`Pipeline` / `ColumnTransformer`)**:

Customer Retention** (`no_purchase_next_90d`) | **80/20 Stratified Holdout Test** + **5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation** on training split (plus Out-of-Time cohort split on Olist `customer_unique_id`). | Dummy Stratified, Logistic Regression (`class_weight='balanced'`) | **PR-AUC** (primary for 34.79% positive class), **ROC-AUC**, F1, Brier Score, Expected Retention Profit | Fit scalers in-fold; exclude post-snapshot variables; group Olist records by `customer_unique_id`. |

## 2. Preliminary Model Monitoring & Covariate Drift Strategy (`model_monitoring_drift_sample.csv`)

Analysis of [`model_monitoring_drift_sample.csv`](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1qLjgQwhkEXhd8rpEuh18RYkeZ23ep_4t/view?usp=drivesdk) (`2024-01-01` to `2024-08-01`) demonstrates why static models degrade without automated drift controls:
- **Severe Positive Drift (>100% Mean Shift)**: `feature_10` rises `+163.70%` (`0.2193` to `0.5783`), `feature_08` rises `+124.41%` (`0.3335` to `0.7484`), and `feature_05` rises `+105.59%` (`0.4023` to `0.8271`, peaking at `0.9868` in July 2024).
- **Moderate Drift**: `feature_02` rises `+45.13%` (`0.4148` to `0.6020`), `feature_07` rises `+36.61%`, while `feature_04` declines `-28.81%` (`0.5296` to `0.3770`).
- **Monitoring Thresholds**: In Module 5, our pipeline will track monthly **Population Stability Index (PSI)** (`PSI >= 0.10` warning, `PSI >= 0.25` critical retraining trigger), **Two-Sample Kolmogorov-Smirnov (KS) tests**, and **Normalized Mean Shift ($|\Delta \mu| / \sigma_{	ext{train}} > 0.50$)** to trigger automated model recalibration.
