## 2. Granular Anomaly & Logical Consistency Findings

### 2.1 Customer Retention Dataset (`customer_retention_features.csv`)
1. **Window Boundary Discrepancies (Recency vs. Frequency)**: Exactly **3,493 records (11.64%)** exhibit `days_since_last_order <= 180` while simultaneously recording `orders_last_180d == 0`. In a real production feature store, this occurs when `orders_last_180d` counts only *completed/settled* orders while `days_since_last_order` references the most recent *checkout attempt* (including canceled or pending orders), or due to independent synthetic feature sampling.
2. **Cross-Window Return Lag**: Exactly **1,039 records (3.46%)** have `orders_last_180d == 0` but `returns_last_180d > 0`, and **1,261 records (4.20%)** have `returns_last_180d > orders_last_180d`. Because returns processed during the 180-day lookback window can originate from purchases made 181–240 days prior, a raw ratio `returns_last_180d / orders_last_180d` will divide by zero or exceed `1.0`. **Remediation**: Define a smoothed return rate `returns_last_180d / (orders_last_180d + 1.0)` and create explicit binary anomaly flags (`flag_return_exceeds_orders`, `flag_recency_zero_order_mismatch`).
3. **Single Snapshot Date Limitation**: All 30,000 rows share a single `snapshot_date` (`2024-01-01`). Consequently, longitudinal survival or multi-cohort temporal validation cannot be performed within the supplementary file alone; stratified out-of-sample splitting must be paired with multi-cohort validation on the [Olist E-Commerce Public Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce).

### 2.2 Delivery Delay Dataset (`delivery_delay_training.csv`)
1. **Physically Impossible Negative Delivery Durations**: While `promised_delivery_days` is strictly positive (`2` to `12` days), `actual_delivery_days` contains **153 strictly negative records (0.38%)** (minimum `-3.1` days) and **178 records `<= 0.0` days (0.45%)**. Because `actual_delivery_days` is a post-dispatch outcome that must be dropped from predictors to prevent target leakage, these negative values do not corrupt dispatch-time features, and their corresponding targets (`delay_days = 0.0`, `late_delivery = 0`) remain valid. However, if `actual_delivery_days` is inspected during historical SLA auditing, records with `actual_delivery_days <= 0` must be flagged as timestamp-inversion clock errors.
2. **Heavy Right-Tail Skew in Physical Attributes**: `package_weight_g` exhibits strong positive skewness (`skew = 2.8293`, `kurtosis = 15.6838`, 2,183 IQR outliers up to `24,215 g`), and `distance_km` exhibits moderate right skewness (`skew = 1.2696`, 1,150 IQR outliers up to `1,800 km`). Tree-based gradient boosters handle monotonic skew natively, whereas linear baselines require `np.log1p()` transformation and `RobustScaler`.

### 2.3 NLP Review Sentiment Datasets (`review_sentiment_training.csv` & `review_sentiment_unlabeled.csv`)
1. **Synthetic Template Collapse**: Profiling reveals that across all 20,000 training rows and 2,000 unlabeled rows, `review_text` takes on **only 12 unique string values** (4 positive, 4 negative, 4 neutral). Every template maps deterministically (100% purity) to a single sentiment label.
2. **Deduplication & Generalization Mandate**: Per [`DATASET_README.md`](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ahrGoKWvUuOwsaCSsiCOVur9QG09ixPl/view?usp=drivesdk), this synthetic corpus is template-based and not representative of natural language. Random row-level train/test splitting leaks identical templates across splits, producing artificial `1.000` accuracy. Our pipeline reports both row-level and deduplicated template-level diagnostics and incorporates free-text customer reviews from `olist_order_reviews_dataset.csv` in the [Olist E-Commerce Public Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce) for realistic NLP evaluation.
